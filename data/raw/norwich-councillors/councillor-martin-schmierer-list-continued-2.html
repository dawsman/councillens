Biography:

Lord Mayor 2018-19

Martin has lived in Norwich since 1994 and was elected as a Green Party councillor for the Mancroft ward in May 2015.

His interests include football, cinema, cooking and hiking.  He would like to help tackle poverty in the city and promote small businesses in his role as a councillor.
