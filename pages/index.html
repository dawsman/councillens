CouncilLens

Early build

CouncilLens shows how your local council's decisions line up with what people asked for, and what actually happened. No jargon. Every claim links to the original document.

What did people ask for? What did the council decide? What actually got done?

The first council and topic are on the way. Until then, you can read how we work and follow progress on GitHub.

CouncilLens is for transparency and public discussion. It does not make legal findings or accuse anyone of wrongdoing.
