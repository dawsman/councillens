What a class can do with this site

Pick a council, open its scorecard, and take one card. Every card names a thing the council said it would do and a thing its own reports say happened. The gap between the two is the lesson.

  • Follow one number to its source. Open How we scored this, click through to the council's own document, and find the figure on the page. Does it say what we say it says?
  • Argue with a grey card. Pick something marked Can't tell. Who would need to publish what, for the answer to exist? Why might it not have been published?
  • Write the rule yourself. Before reading ours, decide how far off budget a council should be before you would call it a miss. Then compare.
  • Separate the fact from the opinion. Take one card and write two sentences: one that only says what the documents say, and one that says what you think about it.
