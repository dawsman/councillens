How is the council doing?

Every line on this page compares one thing the council said it would do with what the council's own later reports say happened. We do not grade it, rank it, or compare it to anywhere else. The council's numbers do the talking.

Who decides what counts as met?

Not us. Every status on this page is worked out by a rule written down in advance, run against the council's own two numbers. The rules are the same for every council on this site, they are published in full, and the page you are reading is generated from the same code that runs them — so the rule described and the rule applied cannot drift apart.
