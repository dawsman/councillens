See how your council listens

Your council publishes a lot: meeting notes, consultations, reports. It is all public, and almost none of it is written for you. CouncilLens gathers it, puts it in plain English, and lines it up so you can see one thing clearly — what people asked for, what the council decided, and what actually happened.

The three questions we answer

Asked

What did people ask for?

Consultation responses, petitions, questions at meetings.

Decided

What did the council decide?

The minute, the vote, the policy that came out of it.

Done

What actually got done?

And where nothing has happened yet, we say so.

What we will and won't claim

The hard part of accountability is knowing whether feedback actually changed a decision. Usually nobody writes that down. Rather than guess, we label every connection, and you can see the evidence behind the label.

🟢 Confirmed
The council's own record says this feedback shaped the decision. We show you the sentence.
🟡 Possible
The feedback came first, but nothing on record proves it caused the decision. So we don't claim it did.
⚪ No link found
We found no connection, and we say so plainly rather than leaving a gap you might fill in yourself.

We would rather tell you we don't know than mislead you. Everything on this site links to the original public document, so you never have to take our word for anything.
